Delivery

Shipping Information

Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. Delivery area and method

We arrange PUDO locker delivery within South Africa, subject to an available, suitable locker and the courier’s service coverage. We confirm your chosen locker and contact details before dispatch. International delivery is not offered through these website offers.

2. Current delivery charges

  • Ordinary fragrance orders: R79 PUDO locker delivery per order, not per bottle. This includes a qualifying October BOGO pair sent together.
  • Single 2ml sample: R49 plus R79 delivery, total R128 when ordered on its own.
  • Discovery Trio: 3 × 2ml for R219, including PUDO locker delivery.
  • Discovery Five: 5 × 2ml for R299, including PUDO locker delivery.

For combined bundles, additional items or an alternative delivery arrangement, we confirm the complete delivered price before payment. Do not assume an additional free delivery or a per-item delivery charge.

3. Preparation and dispatch

Fragrances are blended to order, and some concentrates are ordered for the relevant batch. Before you pay for a WhatsApp order, we confirm availability, the expected dispatch date, the complete order total and your PUDO details. There is no universal 24-hour dispatch guarantee or fixed preparation period for every fragrance.

If timing matters for a gift or event, tell us before payment. We will explain what is feasible rather than promise an unconfirmed arrival date. If availability or agreed timing changes, we will contact you to discuss the next steps and any applicable cancellation or refund rights.

4. How website-to-WhatsApp ordering works

  1. Choose a fragrance on the October offer page, or select exactly three or five fragrances on the discovery sample page.
  2. Review your selection and click the WhatsApp confirmation button.
  3. Check the prefilled message and press Send in WhatsApp. Merely opening WhatsApp does not send the message or place a paid order.
  4. We confirm availability, dispatch timing, your locker and the amount due, then send the secure Yoco payment link.
  5. After payment is confirmed, we prepare and dispatch according to the confirmed arrangement.

The regular website shop also has a Yoco checkout. If you use it, please confirm any time-sensitive delivery requirements with us first; courier timing is not guaranteed by the checkout screen.

5. Transit, tracking and collection

Preparation time and courier transit time are separate. The expected courier transit time depends on your route, dispatch date and the courier’s service conditions; weekends, public holidays and disruptions can affect it. We will provide the available estimate and tracking or collection information when the shipment is arranged.

Use the collection instructions, PIN and deadline supplied for your actual PUDO parcel. Collect within that notified window. We do not promise one fixed locker holding period for every shipment. If a parcel is uncollected, returned or needs redelivery, contact us; we will explain the circumstances and any lawful, agreed additional costs.

6. Incorrect details, loss or damage

Please check your name, phone number and chosen locker carefully. If anything is wrong, contact elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001 as soon as you notice it. We will try to correct it before dispatch. Corrections after dispatch may require courier assistance and any additional cost will be explained.

If a parcel is delayed, missing, damaged or incorrect, contact us so we can investigate with the courier. Photos and tracking details help where available. We do not exclude our legal responsibilities by referring you solely to the courier. See our Returns Policy for remedies and return arrangements.

7. October offer and contact

The October 2026 offer is buy one 100ml fragrance and receive a second 100ml bottle of the same fragrance/formulation free, ending at 23:59 SAST on 31 October 2026. R79 PUDO delivery is additional to the fragrance price. Discovery bundles are separately priced sample offers; they are not automatically part of the 100ml BOGO.

Delivery questions: elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001. Elysean Perfumes is based in George, Western Cape.
