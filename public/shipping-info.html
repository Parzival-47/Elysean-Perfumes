Delivery

Shipping Information

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Delivery area and method

We arrange PUDO locker-to-locker delivery within South Africa, subject to an available, suitable locker and the courier’s service coverage. Festive BOGO customers select their destination locker during checkout. International delivery is not offered through these website offers.

2. Current delivery charges

  • Festive BOGO — 1 to 4 physical bottles: R79 PUDO locker delivery.
  • Festive BOGO — 5 to 8 physical bottles: R104 PUDO locker delivery because the larger locker size is required.
  • Single 2ml sample: R49 plus R79 delivery, total R128 when ordered on its own.
  • Discovery Trio: 3 × 2ml for R219, including PUDO locker delivery.
  • Discovery Five: 5 × 2ml for R299, including PUDO locker delivery.

The Festive checkout calculates its delivery tier automatically from the number of physical bottles. It currently accepts no more than four BOGO sets, which is eight physical bottles, in one online checkout. Contact us before ordering a larger quantity or requesting an alternative arrangement.

3. Preparation and dispatch

Fragrances are blended to order, and concentrates are sourced for the relevant batch. Please allow approximately 7–10 business days for ingredient sourcing, blending and preparation before dispatch. This preparation period excludes PUDO transit after dispatch.

Courier transit depends on the route, locker and service conditions, so we do not promise a fixed delivery date. If timing matters for a gift or event, contact us before paying. If a paid fragrance becomes unavailable or a material delay arises, we will contact you to discuss the available alternatives and any applicable cancellation or refund rights.

4. How Festive BOGO ordering works

  1. Choose an available fragrance on the Festive BOGO page.
  2. Review your BOGO pair or add more qualifying sets, up to four sets per checkout.
  3. Enter your contact information and choose your destination PUDO locker in the checkout.
  4. Review the server-verified price and delivery charge, then continue to Yoco and pay securely.
  5. After Yoco confirms payment, we email your order confirmation and begin sourcing and preparation.

WhatsApp remains available for questions and help, but a WhatsApp conversation is not required for the Festive BOGO checkout. Discovery sample orders currently continue through their own selection and WhatsApp process.

5. Transit, tracking and collection

Preparation time and courier transit time are separate. The expected courier transit time depends on your route, dispatch date and the courier’s service conditions; weekends, public holidays and disruptions can affect it. We will provide the available estimate and tracking or collection information when the shipment is arranged.

Use the collection instructions, PIN and deadline supplied for your actual PUDO parcel. Collect within that notified window. We do not promise one fixed locker holding period for every shipment. If a parcel is uncollected, returned or needs redelivery, contact us; we will explain the circumstances and any lawful, agreed additional costs.

6. Incorrect details, loss or damage

Please check your name, phone number and chosen locker carefully. If anything is wrong, contact elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001 as soon as you notice it. We will try to correct it before dispatch. Corrections after dispatch may require courier assistance and any additional cost will be explained.

If a parcel is delayed, missing, damaged or incorrect, contact us so we can investigate with the courier. Photos and tracking details help where available. We do not exclude our legal responsibilities by referring you solely to the courier. See our Returns Policy for remedies and return arrangements.

7. Festive offer and contact

The Festive 2026 offer is buy one 100ml fragrance and receive a second 100ml bottle of the same fragrance/formulation free, ending at 23:59 SAST on 31 December 2026. PUDO delivery is additional to the fragrance price and is calculated by the applicable bottle tier. Discovery bundles are separately priced sample offers; they are not automatically part of the 100ml BOGO.

Delivery questions: elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001. Elysean Perfumes is based in George, Western Cape.
