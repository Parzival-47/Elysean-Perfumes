GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers
before you order.

Still deciding? You can ask a real person for help on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
Are Elysean fragrances original designer perfumes?

No. Elysean is an independent brand. We create Elysean-branded interpretations inspired by familiar scent profiles; third-party names are references only.

What concentration are the fragrances?

Our full-size fragrances are blended at 20% Eau de Parfum concentration.

Why does preparation take 7–10 business days?

We do not keep every fragrance concentrate as ready-mixed stock. The selected concentrate is sourced for the batch, then your fragrance is blended, bottled and packed.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver locally from George to selected Garden Route areas up to Mossel Bay and Knysna. Free collection in George is also available by arrangement.

How do I pay?

Eligible orders are paid through secure Yoco checkout. Card details are processed by Yoco and are not stored by Elysean.
