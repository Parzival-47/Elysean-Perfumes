BEFORE YOU ORDER

Questions, answered clearly.

Ask on WhatsApp
Can I choose two different fragrances in the BOGO?

No. The complimentary 100ml bottle is the exact same Elysean fragrance and formulation as the 100ml bottle you purchase. The October promotion is one scent × two matching 100ml bottles.

Why is the free bottle the same scent?

Elysean blends to order instead of holding every fragrance concentrate as ready stock. Your selected concentrate is procured for your batch, allowing us to prepare two matching 100ml bottles efficiently and freshly.

When will my order be dispatched?

Because the fragrance concentrate is procured after your order is confirmed, dispatch timing can vary. We confirm the expected dispatch date with you on WhatsApp before sending the Yoco payment link.

How much is delivery?

PUDO locker delivery is R79 nationwide for 100ml orders. Discovery Trio and Discovery Five prices already include PUDO locker delivery.

Are these genuine Dior, YSL, Chanel or other designer perfumes?

No. You are purchasing an Elysean Perfumes product. Third-party designer names are used only as scent references to describe the fragrance profile that inspired our independent interpretation.

How do I pay?

After you select your fragrance, we confirm the exact order and estimated dispatch timing on WhatsApp. We then send you a secure Yoco payment link. You do not need to enter card details into this landing page.

What happens when the October offer ends?

The Buy One 100ml Get the Same 100ml Free promotion ends at 23:59 SAST on 31 October 2026. The page automatically marks the offer as ended after the deadline.
