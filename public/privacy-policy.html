Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. About Elysean and this notice

Elysean Perfumes is an independent fragrance brand based in George, Western Cape, South Africa. This notice covers our website, WhatsApp enquiries, orders and customer service. For privacy questions or requests, contact elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001.

2. Information involved

When you enquire or order, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, fragrance choices, order messages, delivery address or selected PUDO locker, and payment status or transaction reference. Payment card details are entered with Yoco, not into our website tracking events.

Our hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device and access logs to deliver and protect their services. If you allow optional tracking, Google and Meta also receive device/browser information, cookie identifiers, pages visited, campaign information and selected website actions.

3. Why we use information

We use order and contact details to answer questions, confirm availability and dispatch timing, arrange Yoco payment and PUDO delivery, resolve returns and maintain records required for business or legal purposes. Depending on the purpose, processing is based on your request or contract, a legal obligation, a legitimate interest where permitted, or consent. Optional website analytics and advertising tracking use your cookie choices. We do not sell customer lists.

4. Cookies, local storage and your choices

Necessary browser storage keeps your cart, sample selection and cookie preferences. It remains available when you reject optional tracking. Our cookie choice is stored for up to 180 days; you can change it earlier using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

  • Analytics — optional: Google Analytics 4 measures website visits, searches, selections and enquiries. Its cookies commonly start with _ga.
  • Advertising — optional: two Meta Pixels measure the same permitted website actions for Elysean’s advertising accounts. Meta cookies may include _fbp and _fbc. Meta may associate activity with its own identifiers and use it for advertising measurement and delivery.

Neither optional tracking script loads through our website code until you allow its category. Rejecting them does not prevent shopping or WhatsApp ordering. Withdrawing permission stops future events from this website code and attempts to remove its first-party tracking cookies; it does not erase information already received by a provider. Third-party cookie lifetimes and provider retention are governed by their settings and policies.

5. What our event tracking means

With permission, we record actions such as viewing a page or fragrance, searching, filtering, selecting samples, adding to a cart, opening an FAQ, or clicking a contact link. Confirming a completed fragrance or sample selection on WhatsApp records InitiateCheckout (order interest), not a completed payment. This website implementation does not send a Purchase event or report successful Yoco payments to Meta or Google Analytics.

Our custom event code does not send checkout names, email addresses, telephone numbers, delivery addresses, card details or WhatsApp message text. Avoid entering personal information into fragrance searches or campaign links. Analytics and advertising services still process their own technical identifiers.

6. Providers and links

Information is shared where needed with our hosting provider, Yoco for payments, PUDO/the delivery provider for shipping, WhatsApp/Meta for conversations and permitted advertising measurement, Google for permitted analytics and hosted fonts, and Brevo where used for transactional email. These providers may process information outside South Africa, subject to applicable safeguards and their own terms.

Opening WhatsApp, Yoco or another external link takes you to that provider’s service, where its privacy practices apply. See Meta’s Privacy Policy, WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy. Website cookie choices are not permission to send you unrelated marketing messages.

7. Retention and security

Order and conversation records are kept for as long as reasonably needed to fulfil orders, handle support, refunds or disputes and meet applicable record-keeping duties. Provider retention and analytics settings also affect how long information remains with those providers. We use reasonable safeguards; no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to relevant processing, or withdraw consent for future consent-based processing. Some records must be retained for legal reasons. Contact elyseanperfumes@gmail.com or WhatsApp 077 463 8001; we may need proportionate identity verification to protect your information.

You can also lodge a complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator, including through its complaints portal. These rights are informed by the Protection of Personal Information Act.

9. Updates

We will update this notice when our practices change. An updated notice does not, by itself, give us your consent to optional tracking or new marketing uses.
